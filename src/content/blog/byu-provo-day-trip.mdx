If your trip involves a campus visit, a game day, or just a change of scenery, Brigham Young University and Provo make for a solid day trip from Rush Valley — with a scenic backroad option that beats the interstate.

## What to Do on Campus

- **Free 40-minute campus tours** run Monday–Friday, on the hour from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., starting at the visitor center.
- **BYU Museum of Art**, one of the largest museums in the West, with free general admission to its 17,000-plus-piece collection.
- **Monte L. Bean Life Science Museum**, packed with wildlife exhibits and live animal programs — a favorite with kids.
- **Cougar athletics** — football, basketball, and more, depending on the season.

## Tips for the Trip

- Two route options: the faster way runs through Tooele and up I-15; the more scenic option cuts south through **Cedar Fort and Fairfield**, past **Camp Floyd State Park** — a real 1858 army post and Pony Express station worth a quick stop either way (read more in our [Pony Express Trail guide](/blog/pony-express-trail-guide)).
- Campus parking fills up on game days — arrive early or plan to use visitor lots.
- Museum hours vary by day, especially the Museum of Art, which stays open later on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

## How Far Is It From Clover Creek Guest House

About 65–70 miles from Rush Valley, roughly an hour and a half either way you go — a bit farther than Salt Lake City, but the Cedar Fort route makes it feel like part of the adventure rather than a slog on the freeway.
