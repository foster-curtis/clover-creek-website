Temple Square is Salt Lake City's single most-visited attraction, drawing millions of visitors a year to its 35-acre block downtown — and it's an easy, entirely free day trip from Rush Valley.

## What to See

- **The grounds themselves** — manicured gardens, fountains, the new Temple Square Visitor's Center, all free to walk through daily.
- **Guided tours**, offered in more than 40 languages, cover the grounds, the Conference Center, and nearby historic residences.
- **The new Temple Square Visitors' Center**, open daily 9 a.m.–9 p.m. with free admission; the "Inside a Temple" experience requires a reservation.
- **The Tabernacle**, home to the Tabernacle Choir, with regular rehearsals and performances open to the public.

## Tips for Visiting

- Everything on Temple Square is free — the only thing that requires a reservation is the temple-interior experience.
- Parking: the Conference Center offers free parking with validation, and City Creek Center parking gives you two free hours nearby.
- Go early or in the evening in summer to avoid both crowds and heat.
- Pair it with a walk through **City Creek Center** next door for shopping and food.

## How Far Is It From Clover Creek Guest House

About 55 miles — roughly an hour's drive, mostly along UT-36 and I-80 over the Oquirrh Mountains. Close enough for an easy day trip, far enough that you'll come home to real quiet.
