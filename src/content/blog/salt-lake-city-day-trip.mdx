Staying in Rush Valley doesn't mean skipping the city — Salt Lake City is close enough for an easy day trip, and far enough that you'll appreciate coming home to the quiet.

## What to Do

- **Natural History Museum of Utah**, set into the foothills with panoramic city views alongside its exhibits — a great stop for families.
- **Temple Square**, downtown's free, most-visited attraction (see our [dedicated guide](/blog/temple-square-salt-lake-city) for details).
- **This Is the Place Heritage Park**, a living-history village telling the story of Utah's early settlers.
- **Antelope Island State Park**, out in the Great Salt Lake itself, home to free-roaming bison, pronghorn, and bighorn sheep, plus Bridger Bay Beach.
- **Liberty Park and Sugar House**, good options for a relaxed afternoon of food and green space away from downtown.

## Tips for the Day

- Traffic on I-15 through Salt Lake can back up during rush hour (roughly 7–9 a.m. and 4–6 p.m.) — time your drive around it if you can.
- Antelope Island has its own entrance fee and is a bit of a detour north; plan it as its own half-day if you want to do it justice.
- Salt Lake City sits at a noticeably lower, hotter elevation in summer than Rush Valley — dress accordingly.
- The city and Temple Square are both walkable once you're downtown, so park once and explore on foot.
