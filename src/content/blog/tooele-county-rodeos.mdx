If you want a genuine taste of small-town Utah in the summer, a Tooele County rodeo is hard to beat — and the county's biggest ones all happen a short drive from Rush Valley.

## What's On the Calendar

- **Tooele Bit n' Spur Rodeo** — billed as Utah's oldest and Tooele County's largest rodeo, held at the Deseret Peak Complex in [Grantsville](/blog/grantsville-utah-guide) around the Fourth of July, with bull riding, bronc riding, barrel racing, team roping, and fireworks on the 4th.
- **Tooele County Fair's Broncs and Bulls Rodeo** — part of the county fair's Heritage Days celebration in late July/early August, also at Deseret Peak Complex.
- **Grantsville's Memorial Day Rodeo** — a revived local tradition held over Memorial Day weekend.

Exact dates shift year to year, so check current listings before you plan a trip around one.

## Tips for Attending

- **Deseret Peak Complex** in Grantsville is the venue for nearly all of these — plan on that as your destination regardless of which event you're catching.
- Rodeo weekends draw crowds from across the county; arrive early for parking and good seats.
- Evenings cool off fast in the high desert even after a hot afternoon — bring a layer.
- Pair a rodeo trip with the rest of what [Grantsville](/blog/grantsville-utah-guide) has to offer, since you'll already be in town.

## How Far Is It From Clover Creek Guest House

About 30 miles from Rush Valley — roughly a 45-minute drive to the Deseret Peak Complex in Grantsville.
