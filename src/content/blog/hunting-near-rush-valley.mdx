Rush Valley sits right in the middle of Utah's **West Desert hunting country**, with public land units and a well-known private outfitter both within easy reach.

## Public Land Options

- **West Desert/Vernon deer unit** — covers the Sheeprock, Simpson, and West Tinitic mountain ranges nearby, known among Utah hunters as a solid option for mule deer with better-than-average odds of drawing a tag.
- **West Desert pronghorn units** — the open flats and sagebrush country around Rush Valley and Skull Valley hold pronghorn antelope in season.
- **Upland game and small game** — chukar, cottontail, and other species are found throughout the BLM land surrounding the valley.
- Primary access points include Pony Express Road, Cherry Creek Road, and Jericho Junction — most of the unit is reachable by standard vehicle, though high clearance helps on the rougher tracks.

## Private Guided Hunts

If you'd rather hunt with an outfitter, [Royal Creek Ranches](/blog/royal-creek-ranches-rush-valley) is right here in Rush Valley and offers guided elk and pheasant hunts on private land — a fully outfitted alternative to public-land tags and draw odds.

## Tips for Planning a Hunt Here

- **Regulations and seasons change year to year** — always check the current Utah DWR guidebook and unit boundaries before you plan a trip, and confirm your tag/permit through the Utah Hunt Planner.
- Fall weather in the West Desert swings hard between hot afternoons and cold mornings — layer accordingly.
- Water sources are limited; carry more than you think you'll need.
- Cell service is unreliable through much of the unit — let someone know your plans before you head out.
