Tooele isn't a tourist town, and that's exactly why it's useful. It's the largest city in Tooele County and the closest place to Rush Valley for groceries, gas, pharmacies, and hardware — the practical stuff that makes a farmhouse stay in the countryside comfortable instead of a supply-run marathon.

## What to Do There

- **Tooele Valley Museum & Historic Park** (35 N Broadway) — a restored steam locomotive from the old Tooele Valley Railway, mining history exhibits, and a kids' replica underground mine.
- **Tooele Pioneer Museum** — Goshute and pioneer-era artifacts covering the valley's early settlement.
- **Benson Grist Mill** — a restored 1850s mill just outside town, with grounds that make for an easy short stop.
- Views of the **Oquirrh Mountains**, which separate the Tooele Valley from Salt Lake City and give the whole drive in a dramatic backdrop.

## Tips

- Museum hours are limited (afternoons, a few days a week), so check current hours before making a special trip.
- This is your last "real town" stop heading west toward Dugway, the Pony Express Trail, or the Salt Flats — stock up here.
- Downtown Tooele is walkable if you want to stretch your legs between errands.

## How Far Is It From Clover Creek Guest House

About 20 miles from Rush Valley — roughly a 30-minute drive north on UT-36.
