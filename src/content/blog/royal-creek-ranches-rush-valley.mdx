If your trip to Rush Valley involves a wedding, family reunion, corporate retreat, or guided hunt, there's a good chance it's happening at **Royal Creek Ranches** — a large private ranch tucked into the Stansbury Mountains, right here in Rush Valley on Highway 199.

## What's There

Royal Creek Ranches is built around two large lodges: **Pheasant Haven**, with 17 bedrooms sleeping up to 70 guests, and the **Elk Ridge Lodge**, a 9,000-square-foot event venue with a full chef's kitchen and capacity for up to 300 guests. The ranch is known regionally for:

- Guided elk and pheasant hunting
- Horseback riding and cattle sorting
- Fishing and hayrides through the resident elk herd
- Weddings, reunions, and corporate retreats

## Good to Know

- Royal Creek Ranches is a private, event-based venue, not a walk-in tourist attraction — visits are generally by booking or invitation (a wedding, hunt, or retreat).
- If you're one of dozens of guests at a Royal Creek event but want a quieter, more private place to actually sleep, a smaller stay nearby is worth considering (more on that below).
- The ranch's guided hunts are a great option if you're interested in Rush Valley's hunting scene but want it fully outfitted — see our [hunting guide](/blog/hunting-near-rush-valley) for the public-land alternative.

## How Far Is It From Clover Creek Guest House

Practically no distance at all — Royal Creek Ranches sits on the same road as [Clover Creek Guest House](/), just a few minutes' drive away in Rush Valley.
