Grantsville is a small, friendly Tooele County town with an outsized lineup of things to do for its size — from pioneer history to the county's biggest annual events.

## What to Do There

- **Deseret Peak Complex** — the county's event hub, with an aquatic center, BMX and motocross tracks, archery park, equestrian facilities, and the venue for Tooele County's rodeos and fairs (see our [rodeo guide](/blog/tooele-county-rodeos)).
- **Donner-Reed Museum** — artifacts and exhibits tied to western pioneer history, including the ill-fated Donner Party, who passed through this area.
- **Grantsville DUP Museum** — pioneer-era artifacts focused on the women who helped settle the valley.
- **Grantsville Reservoir** — fishing and camping a short drive from town.
- **Bonneville Seabase** — an unusual geothermal dive site a few miles away, with warm mineral water and marine life stocked into a spring-fed pond.

## Tips for Visiting

- Small museums here keep limited hours — call ahead or check online before making a special trip.
- Grantsville's biggest weekends (rodeos, the county fair) draw crowds from across the county — book lodging early if you're visiting during one.
- It's an easy add-on to a Tooele trip; the two towns are about 10 miles apart.
