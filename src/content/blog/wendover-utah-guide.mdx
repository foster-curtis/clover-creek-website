Wendover sits right on the Utah–Nevada border, where a small Utah town gives way to the casino lights of West Wendover across the state line. It's an unusual mix — genuine WWII history on one side, neon on the other — and it makes a memorable stop on a longer West Desert trip.

## What to Do There

- **Historic Wendover Airfield Museum** — the most intact original WWII training airfield left in the country, and where the crew of the *Enola Gay* trained before the mission that ended the war in the Pacific. Self-guided tours take about an hour; a $10 guided tour adds access to the restored B-29 hangar and other areas.
- **West Wendover casinos**, just across the state line in Nevada, for those looking for a bit of nightlife.
- **The Bonneville Salt Flats**, a couple of miles east on I-80 — see our [full Salt Flats guide](/blog/bonneville-salt-flats-guide) for details.

## Tips for Visiting

- The airfield museum has ample free parking and clean restrooms — a good rest stop even if you don't tour the whole thing.
- Time zones change here: Wendover, Utah is Mountain Time, but West Wendover, Nevada is Pacific Time — an hour's difference to keep in mind if you're timing dinner reservations or a show.
- Combine Wendover with the Salt Flats for one big West Desert day rather than two separate trips — they're only a few miles apart.
